Source revisions, dates and file checksums are in the bundled sources.json.

Booking names and codes come from the Duffel snapshot in timrogers/iata-code-decoder-api.
https://github.com/timrogers/iata-code-decoder-api
See UPSTREAM-LICENSE.txt for the upstream project's MIT notice.

Airport coordinates, runways, frequencies, regions and navaids come from OurAirports (public domain).
https://ourairports.com/data/

Carrier and aircraft directories, historical route pairs and airport labels come from OpenFlights.
https://openflights.org/data.php and https://github.com/jpatokal/openflights
OpenFlights-derived profiles are available under the Open Database License 1.0.
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Individual contents are available under the Database Contents License.
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/
Archive counts exclude marked codeshares, stops and unidentified endpoints.

Aircraft dimensions, design weights and classifications come from the FAA Aircraft Characteristics Database.
https://www.faa.gov/airports/engineering/aircraft_char_database
Values describe the named FAA reference model. Individual configurations vary.
